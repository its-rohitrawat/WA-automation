## What it does

- Client and team voice notes are transcribed and the tracker reads them. Voice notes hold up their client's update for at most 30 minutes, and a late transcript re-checks its message.
- **The default engine is Voxtral Mini 3B via transcribe.cpp, running on the Mac** (owner decision after the PC test).
  - It is free, and no audio leaves the computer.
  - Voice notes are off until `VOICE_STT_PROVIDER=local` and the `STT_LOCAL_*` settings are set; see `docs/SETUP-mac.md` section 8.
  - ffmpeg is required: every note is converted and measured first.
- The paid providers stay in the code, optional and off. A paid note can never be paid twice. Meetings are unchanged.
- A failure is never silent:
  - an empty transcript of a note with sound is retried;
  - notes the engine could not transcribe show on the health line and can be retried with `npm run cli -- transcribe-voice --failed`;
  - a missing engine or ffmpeg shows on the health line, and the service keeps running.
- Older voice notes can be transcribed with `npm run cli -- transcribe-voice`: a dry run first, then `--yes`.
