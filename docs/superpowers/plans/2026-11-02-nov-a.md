# Lane A plan: the assistant as its own page (changes 1 and 8)

Branch `feat/nov-a` in `WA_agent-saas-s1`, from `feat/release-nov` at 5bf2a70. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-november-release-design.md`, sections "1 and 8", "Lanes and files", "How each lane works".

## Where it starts

- `views/assistant.js` is a floating button plus a slide-over panel, mounted on sign-in and outside the screen system. The chat lives only in that panel.
- The server (`src/assistant/assistant.ts`) keeps each browser session's last 10 questions and answers in memory. They are dropped on sign-out, on "New chat" or after 2 h.
- When the browser goes away, the answer stops after the Claude call in progress (`gone.abort()` in `routes/assistant.ts`).
- Drafted change cards live in memory per browser session for 10 minutes (`PendingActions`).
- Customers have the assistant switched off: `DASHBOARD_ASSISTANT: false` in `customerProfile.ts`.

## Two pieces, landed one after the other

### Piece 1: the shell and the screen (land early; lane D needs `app.js` and `index.html` next)

1. `router.js`: add `['assistant', 'Assistant', 'spark']` to Workspace, right after Overview. A screen address is `/dashboard/assistant`, and `/dashboard/assistant/<chatId>` for one chat (sub).
2. `index.html`: an "Ask the assistant" button at the bottom of the side menu (`data-go="assistant"`), above Collapse. It shows only its icon when the menu is collapsed, with a tooltip.
3. `app.js`: the floating button and panel go (`mountAssistant`/`unmountAssistant`), and `SCREEN_MOUNTS.set('assistant', …)` comes in. A link in an answer opens a screen through the existing `ASSISTANT_GO` handler.
4. `views/assistant.js` becomes a screen mount. It keeps today's API, with one chat until piece 2, and has the layout ready for the chat list.
5. Leaving the screen aborts the browser's reading of the stream (an `AbortController` on `postLines`) and stops its polls. That is change 8 in the browser.
6. Tests: router (nav entry, its address, a customer's nav), plus any shell tests that name the floating button.

### Piece 2: chats stored per workspace, the chat list, and change 8 on the agent

7. Schema: SQLite **v13** and Postgres **P6**, test-first. Both tables join `CUSTOMER_TABLES`, so P6 runs `rlsSql([...])` for them and the access check covers them.

   | Table | Columns |
   |---|---|
   | `assistant_chats` | id, created_by (email), title (1 to 80 characters), created_at, updated_at |
   | `assistant_messages` | id, chat_id (a composite FK on Postgres), role (user or assistant), body (up to 16,000 characters), status (running, done, stopped or failed), note, cost_usd, cards (JSON), links (JSON), created_at |

8. A new store, `src/store/assistantChats.ts`, built on the Db API only:
   - list (newest first, at most 200);
   - get with messages;
   - create;
   - rename;
   - delete (messages, then the chat, in one transaction);
   - add a question;
   - start an answer, save partial text (at most every 1 s), and finish it;
   - mark a card confirmed or cancelled.
9. `Assistant` works per chat:
   - Its history comes from the stored chat: the last 10 turns, each answer cut to 4,000 characters.
   - A new chat's title is its first question, trimmed and cut to 60 characters.
   - One question runs at a time per chat, and at most 3 per workspace.
   - **The run no longer stops when the browser goes away.** It finishes on the agent and is saved.
   - **Stop** (`POST …/chats/:id/stop`) saves the text so far with "Stopped" and starts no further step. The call in progress finishes and is booked, as today; spend stays honest.
   - A row still marked running when nothing is running (after a restart) is shown as "interrupted".
10. API, under `/dashboard/api/assistant`, every route owner-only, every POST with Origin and CSRF checks:
    - `GET /chats`
    - `GET /chats/:id`: its messages, whether it is running, and which of its cards this session can still Confirm
    - `POST /ask {chatId?, question}`: a new chat when there is no `chatId`; the first stream line is `{type:'chat', id, title}`
    - `POST /chats/:id/rename`
    - `POST /chats/:id/delete`
    - `POST /chats/:id/stop`
    - confirm and cancel, as today, now also recording the card's outcome in the chat
    - `/reset` is removed.

    Chat ids are numbers. Row-level security keeps every workspace to its own rows: an id from another workspace is a 404.
11. The screen:
    - **Left:** New chat, then the past chats grouped Today, Yesterday, Previous 7 days and Older, each with rename and delete (delete asks first).
    - **Middle:** the conversation. Text streams in and goes on the page through `textContent`/`esc` only. Cards keep Confirm and Cancel while they are live.
    - **Bottom:** the composer. Enter sends, Shift+Enter makes a new line, and a Stop button shows while an answer streams.
    - **On a phone:** the chat list is a drawer.
    - Opening a chat that is still answering polls it every 2 s until it is done.
12. Spend and audit stay as today: the paid-call cap and the confirm audit. Chats are not audited (they are the workspace's own notes); deleting one removes its rows.

## Tests (test-first, small commits)

- **Migrations:** the v13 and P6 text; the pin moves from P5 to P6; `CUSTOMER_TABLES` and the access check include both tables; the SQLite and Postgres columns match.
- **Store:** every operation, on both engines. On Postgres, workspace A cannot see or delete B's chats.
- **Assistant:**
  - the history comes from the store;
  - a run continues and is saved after the browser goes away;
  - Stop;
  - one run per chat, three per workspace;
  - the title from the first question.
- **API:**
  - CSRF on every POST;
  - an id from another workspace is a 404;
  - rename and delete;
  - stop;
  - the card outcome;
  - the route sweep (`customerSessions`) still refuses every founder route to a customer.
- **Browser:** the screen at phone and laptop widths, light and dark: the list, the drawer, Stop, a long answer, and an empty workspace.
- **Every gate:** `sendScan`, `dbAccess` and `noBypass`.
