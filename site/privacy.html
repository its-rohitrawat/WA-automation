Who we are

WA Agent is built and run by Solution Bowl. This policy covers this website (waagent.agentsolutionbowl.si) and the WA Agent service that businesses run on their own computer.

What this website collects

  • Demo requests: the name, work email, company and answers you type into the form. We use them only to reply to you.
  • No tracking or advertising cookies. The website sets no cookies of its own.
  • Hosting logs: our host keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and pages requested) to keep the site running and secure.

What the WA Agent service reads

  • WhatsApp: messages, files and voice notes in the client groups the business has approved. Other chats are ignored.
  • Google Meet: the business's meeting recordings, read only, if meeting summaries are switched on.
  • Google Calendar: meeting invites, read only, to match a recording to the right client.
  • Google Drive: only the files the agent creates (the tracker and meeting summaries).

How the data is used

  • To build the business's task tracker and meeting summaries, and nothing else.
  • Message text, file text and meeting transcripts are sent to Anthropic's Claude service to find tasks and write summaries. Meeting recordings may be sent to a speech-to-text service. Voice notes are turned into text on the business's own computer, or, if the business chooses, by a speech-to-text service.
  • No data is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

The owner dashboard

Owners sign in with Google. The dashboard receives only their name and email, to check they are on the owner list. A sign-in cookie keeps them signed in for up to 7 days and is removed when they sign out. Sign-ins and changes are recorded in an audit log on the business's computer.

Google API data

WA Agent's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is stored

  • On the business's own computer, with saved sign-ins encrypted.
  • In the business's own Google Drive (the tracker and the summaries).

Your choices

  • Remove WA Agent's Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Remove its WhatsApp link from the phone: WhatsApp → Linked devices.
  • To have your data deleted, email us.
